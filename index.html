01 — About 01 — 关于我

Researching AI systems that move from prototypes to real-world impact.

从研究原型， 到真正产生现实影响的 人工智能系统。

I am a Research Assistant at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. My work focuses on healthcare AI, large language models, multimodal learning, and intelligent agents. I am particularly interested in systems that combine rigorous AI research with practical deployment.

我目前在香港中文大学（深圳） 担任研究助理。 我的研究主要涉及医疗人工智能、 大语言模型、多模态学习与智能体系统。 我尤其关注如何将具有研究价值的 人工智能方法进一步转化为 可实际部署和使用的系统。

01
Healthcare AI · LLM

EasyMED & SPBench

Developed and evaluated an LLM-based virtual standardized patient framework for medical education. The work introduced SPBench, a benchmark for evaluating standardized patient simulation.

面向医学教育， 设计并验证基于大语言模型的 虚拟标准化病人系统。 构建标准化病人模拟评测基准 SPBench， 系统评估大模型虚拟病人的 真实性与教学效果。

SPBench Benchmark 评测基准

02
Clinical LLM · Deployment

Regional Clinical Triage LLM

区域医疗分诊大模型

Built data pipelines and multi-agent patient simulations for an outpatient triage LLM. Designed dynamic data mixing and supervised fine-tuning strategies, contributing to a system deployed across hospitals in Shenzhen.

构建医疗数据处理流程与 Multi-Agent 医患交互模拟， 用于生成分诊训练数据。 设计动态数据配比与 SFT 微调策略， 最终系统部署至深圳多家医院。

+16% Accuracy 准确率提升
11 Hospitals 医院
70K Monthly users 月均用户
03
Data · LLM Pipeline

Clinical Data Pipeline

临床数据 Pipeline

Designed automated pipelines for extracting structured clinical information from unstructured literature. Processed more than 100,000 clinical cases and constructed virtual patient resources for multiple research projects.

构建自动化病例信息提取 Pipeline， 通过 Prompt Engineering 从非结构化医学文献中提取 结构化病例信息。 共处理超过 10 万例病例， 并构建虚拟病人资源库。

100K+ Cases 病例
98% Accuracy 人工评估准确率
−80% Annotation cost 标注成本
04
Speech AI · Multimodal

Intelligent Voice Assistant

智能语音助手

Designed a low-latency conversational voice application integrating ASR, large language models and text-to-speech. The system supports real-time interaction with sub-600 ms model communication latency.

设计并实现低延迟 实时语音交互系统， 集成 ASR、大语言模型与 TTS。 系统实现与大模型 低于 600ms 的实时通讯。
