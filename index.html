01 — About

Researching AI systems that move from prototypes to real-world impact.

I am a Research Assistant at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. My research interests include healthcare AI, large language models, multimodal learning, and intelligent agents. I am particularly interested in building AI systems that combine rigorous research with practical deployment, from virtual standardized patients and clinical reasoning systems to multimodal and conversational AI applications.

02 — Selected Work

Selected research & projects

01
Healthcare AI · LLM

EasyMED & SPBench

Developed and evaluated an LLM-based virtual standardized patient framework for medical education. The work introduced SPBench, a benchmark for standardized patient simulation, and compared LLM virtual patients with human standardized patients.

SPBench Benchmark
ACL 2026 Publication
02
Clinical LLM · Deployment

Regional Clinical Triage LLM

Built data pipelines and multi-agent patient simulations for an outpatient triage model. Designed dynamic data mixing strategies and performed supervised fine-tuning, contributing to a production system deployed across hospitals in Shenzhen.

Data · LLM Pipeline

Clinical Data Pipeline

Designed automated pipelines for extracting structured clinical information from unstructured medical literature. Processed more than 100,000 clinical cases and constructed virtual patient resources supporting multiple research projects.

Speech AI · Multimodal

Agentic AI · Multimodal

Medical Video Generation Agent

Designed an automated medical science communication video generation agent and deployed the system through FastMCP. The pipeline connects medical scripts with downstream media generation tools to automate the production process.

2×+ Production efficiency
06
Multi-Agent · RAG

Agentic Clinical Applications

Developed Text2SQL, Agentic RAG, TTS and ASR components for clinical reasoning applications. Built multi-agent workflows using DeerFlow, LangGraph and ReAct, covering planning, tool execution, multi-turn interaction and report generation.
